Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This is a plain-English summary of how Silak Salapi handles your data. If anything here isn't clear, email us at silak.ph@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect your email address and password (encrypted by our authentication provider). Everything else you enter — transactions, wallets, budgets, savings goals, loans, tags, categories, assets, notes — stays yours and is stored in your personal account.

We do not collect analytics, ad IDs, contacts, location, device identifiers, or usage tracking data.

Where your data is stored

Your account and data live in a database managed by Supabase (region: South Asia). Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Data at rest is encrypted at the storage layer.

Who can see your data

Only you (via your login) and the app operator (for support / debugging when you email us). We do not sell, rent, share, or use your data for advertising. We will only disclose data when compelled by a lawful court order.

Your rights

  • Export — download all your data anytime from Profile → Export all data
  • Delete — wipe your account and all data from Profile → Delete my account
  • Correct — edit any transaction, budget, or profile detail directly in the app
  • Ask — email silak.ph@gmail.com with any privacy question

Cookies & local storage

Silak Salapi uses your browser's local storage to remember your login session and cache backup snapshots. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party pixels.

Children

Silak Salapi is not intended for users under 13. If you're a parent and believe your child created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy in ways that materially affect what data we collect or how we use it, we'll let you know via the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions, concerns, requests: silak.ph@gmail.com
